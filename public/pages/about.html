About

Age Rating: 13+
Genres: Drama, comedy, supernatural
Content Warnings: Minor foul language, some adult themes

When a no-nonsense, skeptical, but promising young band director is hired by a secret government organization called IBAPI, he is tasked with overseeing their hidden concert band ensemble...made entirely of sentient human instruments. Not much is known about these humanoid entities but according to IBAPI's studies, even the youngest of them have been around for hundreds of years. The new band director, simply known as Conductor, must keep his job and the existance of these "instrumentum" a secret from the outside world, or risk a widespread and global manhunt for the instrumentum. After all, humans fear what they do not understand...

Area B-Flat is a webcomic created by weywyrd! Inspired by works such as Steven Universe and Hetalia, Area B-Flat features personified musical instruments and the weird government organization that keeps them secret!
